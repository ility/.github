# Ility Labs

### Ility Labs creates tools that support the people making digital experiences accessible to everyone.

---

**Ility is an accessibility management platform that helps teams discover, understand, prioritize, and resolve digital accessibility barriers.**

Automated accessibility testing is an important starting point, but identifying violations is only part of the challenge. Making meaningful improvements requires context, collaboration, and a practical path toward remediation.

We're building Ility to bridge that gap.

**[Visit IlityLabs.com](https://ilitylabs.com)**

## What we're building

Ility brings accessibility testing, issue management, and remediation workflows together in one platform.

Our focus includes:

- **Continuous accessibility monitoring** — Identify accessibility issues across entire websites, not just individual pages.
- **Actionable insights** — Help developers understand why an issue matters and how to address it.
- **Intelligent remediation** — Use AI to provide contextual guidance and accelerate accessibility improvements.
- **Collaborative workflows** — Give teams a shared place to assign, discuss, prioritize, and resolve accessibility issues.
- **Accountability and progress** — Maintain a history of accessibility improvements and ongoing efforts.

Our goal isn't to build another tool that simply identifies more problems.

**We want to make fixing accessibility issues easier.**

## Our approach to accessibility

We believe accessibility is an ongoing commitment, not a checklist.

Automated testing plays an important role, but it cannot replace human judgment, assistive technology testing, or the lived experiences of people with disabilities.

We believe that:

- Accessibility should be considered throughout the entire lifecycle.
- Teams need actionable guidance, not just lists of violations.
- People with disabilities should have a meaningful voice in how accessible experiences are designed and evaluated.
- AI can support accessibility professionals and developers, but it cannot replace human expertise.
- Accessibility improvements should be measurable, collaborative, and sustainable.

Ultimately, accessibility is about people, not compliance scores.

## Built for the people doing the work

We're designing Ility for the designers, developers, accessibility professionals, agencies, and organizations responsible for making digital experiences accessible.

Whether you're fixing your first WCAG violation or managing accessibility across thousands of pages, we want to help make the process more manageable.
